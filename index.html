Privacy Policy

Upkeep - Home Maintenance Tracker by PlannedAndPaid

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version: Your data never leaves your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Introduction

Upkeep ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by PlannedAndPaid, is a home maintenance tracker app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in Upkeep is stored locally on your device only:

  • Home details you enter (names, addresses)
  • Appliance and system information (names, brands, model numbers, serial numbers, purchase dates, warranty information)
  • Maintenance tasks, completion records, costs, and notes

This data is stored in your device's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no access to it, and we could not read it even if we wanted to.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No crash reporting sent to us.
  • No advertising identifiers collected.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit any personal information, because we never receive any.

Subscriptions (Upkeep Pro)

Upkeep Pro ($19.99/year) is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. When you subscribe, Google handles the payment and shares only the subscription status with the app so it can unlock Pro features. Google's handling of payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not see or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable maintenance reminders, they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by the Android operating system. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Data export and deletion

  • Export: Pro users can export their data to CSV files saved on their device (Settings > Export Data).
  • Delete: You can permanently delete all app data at any time from Settings > Delete All Data. This wipes everything stored by the app on your device. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

Upkeep is not directed at children under 13, and we collect no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
